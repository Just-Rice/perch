    Confirm it's you

    Use your passkey for your PC: Face ID, Windows Hello, your fingerprint, your screen lock or a phone nearby.

    Pair this device

    Your PC your PC is ready. Make a passkey for it on this device, then type the code from your authenticator app.

    • The passkey is saved in this device's password manager (such as iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or Windows Hello).
    • Next time, just open Roost and pick your PC.

    Only pair your own devices. Roost gives full control of the PC. If someone sent you this link, close this page.

    Type your code

    Open your authenticator app and type the 6-digit code for Roost · your PC.

    It changes every 30 seconds. Pasting works too.

    Sign in another way

    If backup sign-in is turned on for your PC, use the PC's Roost name and the backup password you chose in the Control Center. You'll still need the code from your authenticator app.

      Keyboard

      ⌘C copies and ⌘V pastes on the PC, as on a Mac. Control becomes the Windows key.

      Mouse and touch

      Your finger moves the PC's pointer like a laptop's touchpad; tap to click where the pointer is.
